Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Files processed in your browser

Video and audio files selected in the Reels Editor are processed in your browser for preview and export. The editor is not designed to upload those selected media files to GetAutoReel servers. Temporary browser media URLs are used during editing and may be cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.

Third-party services and links

The tool directory links to websites operated by other companies. When you open an external provider's website, that provider controls its own collection and use of information under its own privacy policy. Tool icons are requested from Google's favicon service; the browser may send standard connection information such as IP address and browser details when loading those images.

Advertising and cookies

The Google AdSense code is installed on this website for publisher account and site verification. Ads are not currently being displayed. If Google approves the site and advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies, IP addresses and device or browser information to serve and measure ads and help prevent invalid activity, subject to applicable settings and law. Visitors can review Google's information about how it uses data on partner sites and manage available ad settings. This policy will be updated if advertising, analytics or other data practices change.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and review Google Ads Settings.

Contact and data safety

For technical website feedback, use the Contact page. The issue tracker is public; do not post sensitive personal information, passwords, payment details or private media files.
